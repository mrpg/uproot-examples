Cards and list groups put related content into a visible box. Cards suit self-contained units, such as the options of a choice, the parts of a study, or a partner’s profile. List groups suit sequences of short items, such as steps, earnings, or a checklist.

Anatomy of a card

A card is a .card with up to three parts: an optional .card-header, a .card-body, and an optional .card-footer. The body arranges its children in a column with even gaps, so headings and paragraphs need no margin classes. .card-subtitle reduces the gap above a subtitle; .card-title and .card-text add no styles but are useful names to find elements by. For a card without header and footer, use the shortcut class="card card-body".

Key classes: .card, .card-header, .card-body, .card-subtitle, .card-footer

Colors

A theme class colors a whole card: .card .theme-primary gets a colored border and a solid header. Add .card-subtle for calmer colors, which are good for status. Borders and backgrounds can also be set individually with utilities such as .border-success.

Key classes: .theme-*, .card-subtle, .border-*

Grids of cards with equal heights

To show options side by side, put cards into a grid: .row-cols-1 .md:row-cols-3 stacks them on phones and shows three per row from tablets on. Cards in a row only have equal heights with .h-100. .card-footer then sits at the bottom of each card, even if the bodies have different lengths.

Key classes: .row-cols-*, .g-*, .h-100

Horizontal cards

.card-row arranges the parts of a card side by side, for example an image (with .card-img-start) or an avatar next to the text. .overflow-hidden clips a colored panel to the card’s rounded corners.

Key classes: .card-row, .card-img-start, .card-img-end

List groups

A list group is a bordered list. Items can carry a badge on the right (with .d-flex .justify-content-between), be numbered (.list-group-numbered on an <ol>), be colored with a theme class, or be marked as active or disabled. .list-group-flush removes the outer borders, so that a list fits seamlessly into a card.

Key classes: .list-group, .list-group-item, .list-group-numbered, .list-group-flush, .theme-*, .active, .disabled, .md:list-group-horizontal, .contains-inline
