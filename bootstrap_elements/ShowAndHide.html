Long pages overwhelm participants. The components in this chapter keep a page short by showing content only on request: details behind a button, answers in an accordion, sections in tabs, or instructions in a side panel. They all work with data-bs-* attributes alone, without writing JavaScript.

Pitfall. Participants skip what they do not see. Never hide information that everyone must read, and remember that hidden content may differ in how often it is read across treatments. If that matters, record what participants open; the accordion below shows how to listen for it.

Collapse

A collapse shows or hides one element. The trigger, a button with data-bs-toggle="collapse", points to the element with data-bs-target. aria-expanded and aria-controls tell screen readers what the button does; Bootstrap keeps aria-expanded up to date.

Key classes and attributes: .collapse, .show (initially open), data-bs-toggle="collapse", data-bs-target

Your payoff is your endowment minus your contribution plus your share of the group account.

Suppose you contribute $5 and the group account contains $40 in total. With an MPCR of 0.4, your payoff is $20 − $5 + 0.4 × $40 = $31.

Accordions

An accordion is a stack of collapsible items, ideal for frequently asked questions. With data-bs-parent on each item’s collapse, opening one item closes the others; without it, items open independently. .accordion-flush removes the outer borders.

Key classes: .accordion, .accordion-item, .accordion-header, .accordion-button, .accordion-collapse, .accordion-body, .accordion-flush

Tabs and pills

Tabs show one of several panes. Each tab is a button with data-bs-toggle="tab" (or "pill") that points to a .tab-pane. Pills are tabs that look like buttons. .nav-fill stretches the tabs across the full width. If questions are spread over tabs and must all be answered, use the approach of the tabs app, which takes participants to the tab with an error.

Key classes: .nav, .nav-tabs, .nav-pills, .nav-fill, .nav-link, .tab-content, .tab-pane, .fade, .show, .active

Carousels

A carousel shows one slide at a time, for example instructions as a slideshow. For experiments, do not let it advance on its own: participants should control the pace. Bootstrap only autoplays with data-bs-ride="carousel", so simply leave that attribute out. data-bs-wrap="false" stops at the last slide instead of starting over, and data-bs-theme="dark" darkens the controls for slides with a light background.

Key classes and attributes: .carousel, .slide, .carousel-inner, .carousel-item, .carousel-indicators, .carousel-control-prev, .carousel-control-next, data-bs-wrap

Offcanvas panels

An offcanvas panel slides in from the edge of the screen. It is a good place for instructions that participants may want to look up again on later pages, without cluttering those pages. Choose the edge with .offcanvas-start, .offcanvas-end, .offcanvas-top, or .offcanvas-bottom.

Key classes and attributes: .offcanvas, .offcanvas-end, .offcanvas-header, .offcanvas-title, .offcanvas-body, data-bs-toggle="offcanvas", data-bs-dismiss="offcanvas"

Breadcrumbs

On websites, breadcrumbs are links to the parent pages. In a study, where participants cannot jump between pages, plain text items still show where they are among the parts of the study. The separator can be changed with the CSS variable --bs-breadcrumb-divider.

Key classes: .breadcrumb, .breadcrumb-item, .active

Pagination

Bootstrap’s .pagination is just the look of page numbers. uproot comes with a small helper that does the work: give a container the class .uproot-pagination and its direct children the class .uproot-pagination-item, and uproot shows them in pages and adds the controls. data-items-per-page sets the page size (default 8).

Key classes and attributes: .uproot-pagination, .uproot-pagination-item, data-items-per-page; for the looks: .pagination, .page-item, .page-link, .pagination-sm
