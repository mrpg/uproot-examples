Long pages overwhelm participants. The components in this chapter keep a page short by showing content only on request: details behind a button, answers in an accordion, sections in tabs, or instructions in a drawer. Most of them work with data-bs-* attributes alone, and accordions need no JavaScript at all.

Pitfall. Participants skip what they do not see. Never hide information that everyone must read, and remember that hidden content may differ in how often it is read across treatments. If that matters, record what participants open; the accordion below shows how to listen for it.

Collapse

A collapse shows or hides one element. The trigger, a button with data-bs-toggle="collapse", points to the element with data-bs-target. aria-expanded and aria-controls tell screen readers what the button does; Bootstrap keeps aria-expanded up to date.

Key classes and attributes: .collapse, .show (initially open), data-bs-toggle="collapse", data-bs-target

Your payoff is your endowment minus your contribution plus your share of the group account.

Suppose you contribute $5 and the group account contains $40 in total. With an MPCR of 0.4, your payoff is $20 − $5 + 0.4 × $40 = $31.

Accordions

An accordion is a stack of collapsible items, ideal for frequently asked questions. Each item is a native <details> element with a <summary>, so the browser does the work and no JavaScript is needed. Items that share a name attribute form a group in which opening one item closes the others; without it, items open independently. The open attribute opens an item initially. .accordion-flush removes the outer borders, and a theme class colors the open item.

Key classes and attributes: .accordion, <details class="accordion-item" name="…">, <summary class="accordion-header">, .accordion-icon, .accordion-body, .accordion-flush, .theme-*

Tabs and pills

Tabs show one of several panes. Each tab is a button with data-bs-toggle="tab" that points to a .tab-pane; the pane fades in by itself. Pills are tabs that look like buttons. .nav-fill stretches the tabs across the full width. If questions are spread over tabs and must all be answered, use the approach of the tabs app, which takes participants to the tab with an error.

Key classes: .nav, .nav-tabs, .nav-pills, .nav-fill, .nav-link, .tab-content, .tab-pane, .show, .active

Carousels

A carousel shows one slide at a time, for example instructions as a slideshow. Participants can also swipe or scroll through the slides, because the carousel is a native scroll container. For experiments, do not let it advance on its own: participants should control the pace. Bootstrap only autoplays with data-bs-autoplay="true", so simply leave that attribute out. data-bs-ends="stop" stops at the last slide instead of starting over and disables the Previous and Next buttons at the ends.

The controls are ordinary buttons with data-bs-slide, placed anywhere inside the .carousel. A carousel without autoplay must be initialized once in JavaScript, so that the indicators and the disabled buttons follow along when participants swipe (see the late block of this template).

Key classes and attributes: .carousel, .carousel-inner, .carousel-item, .carousel-indicators, .carousel-icon-prev, .carousel-icon-next, data-bs-slide, data-bs-ends

Drawers

A drawer slides in from the edge of the screen. It is a good place for instructions that participants may want to look up again on later pages, without cluttering those pages. A drawer is a native <dialog> element: while it is open, the rest of the page cannot be used, and keyboard focus stays inside. Choose the edge with .drawer-start, .drawer-end, .drawer-top, or .drawer-bottom.

Key classes and attributes: <dialog class="drawer">, .drawer-end, .drawer-header, .drawer-title, .drawer-body, data-bs-toggle="drawer", data-bs-dismiss="drawer"

Breadcrumbs

On websites, breadcrumbs are links to the parent pages. In a study, where participants cannot jump between pages, plain text items still show where they are among the parts of the study. Separators are .breadcrumb-divider elements between the items: an empty one shows a chevron. Hide them from screen readers with aria-hidden="true", since they carry no information.

Key classes: .breadcrumb, .breadcrumb-item, .breadcrumb-divider, .breadcrumb-link (for links)

Pagination

Bootstrap’s .pagination is just the look of page numbers. uproot comes with a small helper that does the work: give a container the class .uproot-pagination and its direct children the class .uproot-pagination-item, and uproot shows them in pages and adds the controls. data-items-per-page sets the page size (default 8).

Key classes and attributes: .uproot-pagination, .uproot-pagination-item, data-items-per-page; for the looks: .pagination, .page-item, .page-link, .pagination-sm
